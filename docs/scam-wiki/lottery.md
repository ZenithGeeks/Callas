# Lottery / prize

The victim is told they won a prize, but must pay first.

## Caller says the victim won a prize and must pay a fee or tax before receiving it
- source: general public warnings (seed example)
- published:
- active: yes

Tactics:
- Says the victim was picked in a draw they never entered, run by a well-known company.
- Names a large prize such as a car or cash, and asks for ID details to "register" the win.
- Requires a transfer fee, tax or insurance payment before the prize can be sent.
- Adds new fees after each payment, with a deadline for each.

Phrases:
- ยินดีด้วยค่ะ คุณ {RECEIVER_NAME} ได้รับรางวัลเงินสด {MONEY} จาก {ORG}
- ก่อนรับรางวัล ต้องชำระค่าภาษีก่อน โอนเข้า {BANK_ACCOUNT}
- สิทธิ์นี้จะหมดอายุภายในวันนี้ค่ะ
