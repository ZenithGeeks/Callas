# Phishing / OTP

Callers pose as a bank or service and try to get an OTP, a PIN or card details.

## Caller from bank security says the account is at risk and asks for the OTP
- source: general public warnings (seed example)
- published:
- active: no

Tactics:
- Calls as bank security and says there is a suspicious transaction on the victim's account.
- Sends a real OTP message to the victim's phone, then asks the victim to read it out to "cancel" the transaction.
- Creates urgency: the account will be frozen in minutes if the victim does not act.
- Sometimes sends a link to a fake bank page that asks for the PIN or card number.

Phrases:
- ธนาคารตรวจพบรายการผิดปกติ ขอรหัส OTP ที่ส่งไปยกเลิกรายการค่ะ
- บัญชีของคุณจะถูกระงับภายใน 10 นาที
- กรุณากรอกข้อมูลบัตรที่ {URL}

## Caller poses as the Department of Business Development and asks the victim to update business registration data via a fake online link.
- source: https://www.antifakenewscenter.com/?p=53870
- published: 2024-03-17
- active: yes

Tactics:
- Call the victim or send a fake official letter via social media
- Claim that the business registration data needs to be updated urgently
- Direct the victim to a fake website resembling the official DBD site
- Ask for personal or business details to 'complete the update'

Phrases:
- กรมพัฒนาธุรกิจการค้า ให้ทำการอัปเดตข้อมูลนิติบุคคลผ่าน DBD e-Service

## Caller poses as an official, claiming the victim's pension has a problem or needs urgent verification, to trick them into revealing data or clicking a fake link.
- source: https://www.antifakenewscenter.com/?p=88697
- published: 2026-06-23
- active: yes

Tactics:
- Call the victim claiming to be an official
- State that the pension has a problem or needs urgent verification
- Use the name of a trusted agency to build credibility
- Ask the victim to reveal important data or click a fake link

Phrases:
- ต้องยืนยันข้อมูลด่วน

## Fake HomePro account staff contact via LINE to offer refunds or compensation, asking for OTP or PIN.
- source: https://www.antifakenewscenter.com/?p=86301
- published: 2026-04-25
- active: no

Tactics:
- Contact victims via LINE posing as HomePro staff
- Claim to refund or compensate for service issues
- Ask for personal details, bank account number, and PIN/OTP
- Use credentials to drain the bank account
