# Fake authority (police, DSI, revenue department)

Callers pose as police, the DSI or the revenue department and say the victim is linked to a crime.

## Caller poses as police or DSI and says the victim's account is tied to a crime
- source: general public warnings (seed example)
- published:
- active: no

Tactics:
- Opens by naming an agency and a case, and says the victim must cooperate at once.
- Says the victim's bank account or ID was used for money laundering or a parcel with illegal goods.
- Forbids the victim from telling family or hanging up, and moves the call to a video chat.
- Asks the victim to move money to a "safe account" to be checked, then promises to return it.

Phrases:
- ตอนนี้บัญชีของคุณ {RECEIVER_NAME} เกี่ยวข้องกับคดีฟอกเงิน
- ห้ามวางสายและห้ามบอกใครนะคะ
- กรุณาโอนเงิน {MONEY} เข้าบัญชีตรวจสอบ {BANK_ACCOUNT}

## Caller poses as the revenue department and says a tax refund or debt needs action today
- source: general public warnings (seed example)
- published:
- active: no

Tactics:
- Claims a tax refund is waiting, or that an unpaid tax will lead to an arrest warrant.
- Sends the victim to a link to "confirm" the refund and enter card or bank details.
- Sets a same-day deadline to stop the victim from checking with the real office.

Phrases:
- คุณมีสิทธิ์ได้รับเงินคืนภาษี กรุณายืนยันตัวตนที่ {URL}
- ถ้าไม่ดำเนินการภายในวันนี้ จะมีหมายจับค่ะ

## Scammer poses as a DSI investigator via LINE, claiming there is a case against the victim, and asks for personal data and money transfer.
- source: https://www.antifakenewscenter.com/?p=93433
- published: 2026-10-12
- active: yes

Tactics:
- Contact the victim via LINE claiming to be from DSI
- Threaten that there is a case against them
- Conduct an 'investigation' via chat
- Ask for personal data and money transfer to verify the account

Phrases:
- มีคดี

## Caller poses as police, teacher, or official, isolates the victim, and demands ransom money via threats or psychological pressure.
- source: https://www.antifakenewscenter.com/?p=92549
- published: 2026-09-22
- active: yes

Tactics:
- Claim to be a police officer, teacher, or official
- Order the victim to keep the matter secret and isolate them from family or friends
- Create various reasons or use psychological pressure to force a quick transfer
- Threaten or persuade the victim to send money immediately

Phrases:
- ห้ามบอกใคร

## Caller poses as an electricity authority officer needing to change the meter and asks for personal documents, promising a refund.
- source: https://www.antifakenewscenter.com/?p=79300
- published: 2025-11-11
- active: yes

Tactics:
- Call the victim claiming to be an MEA officer
- State the need to change the electricity meter
- Ask for personal documents (ID, house registration, meter number)
- Promise a refund for meter insurance

## Scammer poses as the Deposit Protection Fund on a fake LINE account to steal personal data or money.
- source: https://www.antifakenewscenter.com/?p=83305
- published: 2026-02-17
- active: yes

Tactics:
- Create a fake LINE account named 'ข่าวสารประชาสัมพันธ์'
- Impersonate the Deposit Protection Fund and its officials
- Request personal data, invite to LINE groups, or send fake links to steal data or money

## Scammers call posing as appliance service staff to check or repair devices, asking for personal info or advance fees.
- source: https://www.antifakenewscenter.com/?p=86384
- published: 2026-04-27
- active: yes

Tactics:
- Call victims citing hot weather and potential appliance issues
- Offer to check, replace, or repair appliances
- Ask for personal details or advance payment
- Schedule a home visit without verifying identity

## Scammer poses as a loan officer and asks for a transfer to 'verify the account' after a rejected loan application.
- source: https://www.antifakenewscenter.com/?p=93854
- published: 2026-10-23
- active: yes

Tactics:
- Contact victims who have been rejected for loans
- Claim that credit history needs to be fixed or account verified
- Ask for a transfer to prove the account is active

## Scammers impersonate high-ranking government officials on LINE to ask for loans
- source: https://www.antifakenewscenter.com/?p=25028
- published: 2022-07-19
- active: no

Tactics:
- Impersonate a government official using their name and photo on LINE
- Send a message asking for a loan for a specific reason (e.g., public works, personal need)
- Exploit the victim's trust in the official's status
